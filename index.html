For years, I struggled to connect — with people, relationships, even with myself. I spent thousands in therapy with little success… until I discovered the three words in the Torah that changed my life.

From these words came the 12 Core Values I now teach — values that reshape the mind, bring emotional safety, create real connection, and rebuild life from the inside out. This journey became something new. Not therapy. Not theory. Something the world hasn’t experienced before.
